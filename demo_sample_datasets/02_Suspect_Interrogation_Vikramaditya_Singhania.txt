Farhan regarding the Gurugram land dispute is completely preposterous. My attorneys have all boarding passes.

The vehicle has been parked in my Gurugram farmhouse garage with the keys locked in the office safe 
the entire week. Perhaps someone forged a duplicate number plate or your cameras have captured an identical 
vehicle. I was 2,000 kilometers away in the United Arab Emirates.
