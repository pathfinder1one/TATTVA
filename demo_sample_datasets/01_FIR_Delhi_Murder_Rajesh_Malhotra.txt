4. INCIDENT DETAILS & SUMMARY OF OFFENCE:
   On 12-Oct-2024 at approximately 22:30 hours, victim Rajesh Malhotra was ambushed in his vehicle 
   (Mercedes Benz DL-1C-BA-4412) as the private gates were opening. Two unknown helmet-wearing assailants 
   on a dark Pulsar motorcycle forced open the driver-side door and executed multiple stab wounds to the victim's 
   upper chest and carotid artery before fleeing towards the Mehrauli-Gurgaon (MG) Road.
   
   Victim succumbed to injuries en route to Fortis Hospital Vasant Kunj. Attending surgeon reported a deep 
   penetrating wound of approximately 12cm depth characterized by a distinct 4-notch serrated laceration pattern.

5. SUSPECTED MOTIVE & PRIME PERSONS OF INTEREST:
   - Primary Dispute: Victim was locked in high-stakes litigation over a 45-acre commercial land parcel on 
     Gurugram-Faridabad Expressway valued at Rs 320 Crores with real estate tycoon Vikramaditya Singhania.
   - Threat History: Victim filed formal non-cognizable reports alleging extortion threats from Vikramaditya 
     Singhania and Dubai-based hawala broker Farhan Qureshi demanding withdrawal of arbitration proceedings.
   - Vehicle Sighting: Private CCTV from farmhouse gate captured a black Toyota Land Cruiser (Registration: 
     DL-3C-AZ-9901) registered to Singhania Enterprises idling 200 meters away minutes prior to the assault.

6. RECOVERED FORENSIC MATERIAL:
   - Physical: 1x Torn ballistic nylon blade sheath recovered from the hedge.
   - Digital: Gate CCTV DVR hard drive seized (Hash sealed under Delhi Police chain of custody).
   - Forensic Lab Dispatch: Request sent to CFSL New Delhi for blade striation profiling.
